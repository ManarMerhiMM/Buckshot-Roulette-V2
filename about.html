Overview

Two players sit down with one shotgun, a chamber of live and blank shells in an order neither of them knows, and a handful of items. Each round is a straight test of probability, nerve, and how well you can bluff someone across the table.

This started as a terminal game — a Python console app played entirely in text. This is the same game for the most part, rebuilt for the browser, no install required.

Core Gameplay

  • A randomized sequence of live and blank shells loads into the chamber each round
  • On your turn, shoot yourself or shoot your opponent
  • Play multiple rounds — scores carry across the match
  • Whoever dies first loses the round!

Items

Between shells, you might draw an item. Used well, any of these can turn a round around.

🪚

Saw

Doubles the damage of your next shot.

🔍

Magnifying Lens

Reveals whether the next shell is live or blank.

📱

Phone

Reveals a random shell somewhere further down the chamber.

🍺

Beer

Racks the next shell out of the chamber, unfired.

🚬

Smoke

Heals a small amount of health.

💊

Deadly Pill

A gamble — 60% chance to lose 1 health, 40% chance to gain 2.

⛓️

Chains

Restricts your opponent's next turn.

🔄

Inverter

Flips the next shell from live to blank, or blank to live.

Settings & Match History

Health range, item counts, and player colors are all configurable before a match. Global Stats & Match History can be checked at any time!.

Game Mechanics

  • Live shell (💥) — causes damage on a hit
  • Blank shell (🎭) — no damage; shooting yourself with a blank keeps your turn
  • The shell order is hidden, but items can partially reveal it
  • Every item exists to shift the odds, disrupt your opponent's plan, or buy you information

Notes

  • Local 1v1 — both players share the same screen
  • No networking and no AI opponent, by design
